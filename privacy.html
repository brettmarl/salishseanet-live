Salish Seanet app for iPhone and Android

App privacy.

The app collects no personal information, has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and doesn't track you.

  • Your location is used only on your phone, while Look is open, to work out where each vessel is from where you stand. It is never sent anywhere.
  • The camera picture is shown on your screen and nothing else: it isn't recorded, saved or sent.
  • Your Lookout is found and talked to over your own Wi-Fi. Setting one up sends your Wi-Fi name and password over Bluetooth to that Lookout only, which keeps them so it can connect.
  • Vessel lookups: when you tap a vessel, the app asks Open Waters AIS about it by its public identity number (MMSI), and fetches its photo from Wikimedia Commons. Nothing about you is included. What comes back is kept on your phone for a week.
  • The map is drawn by Mapbox, which supplies the map images your phone downloads.

Questions about privacy: hello@salishseanet.org.
