Seanet Lookout

You've found a Seanet Lookout.

This small receiver listens to the radio signals that ships and boats broadcast about themselves: who they are, where they are, and where they're heading. It's part of Salish Seanet, a network of Lookouts hosted by volunteers around the Salish Sea.

See what it hears, in the Salish Seanet app.

Every boat this Lookout picks up, on a list and a live map, with photos and details. Or hold up your phone and see their names right over the water.
